How V3.1 works

  • Your financial cycle runs from the salary date, normally the 25th, to the 24th of the next month.
  • Salary + previous cycle leftover + other income create the money available for the cycle.
  • Recurring commitments repeat indefinitely; installments repeat for a fixed number of cycles and then stop automatically.
  • Visa, 3oshour, spontaneous spending and your savings contribution are entered per cycle.
  • Revolut savings is a cash outflow for budgeting but remains tracked separately toward the €72,000 university goal.
  • Cloud sync is local-first and offline-capable; conflict notices are raised instead of silently hiding detected changes.
  • The leftover at the end of a cycle becomes the next cycle's carry-in.
