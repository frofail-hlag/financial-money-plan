How the app thinks

  • Income comes first.
  • Fixed commitments are reserved.
  • Savings target is reserved before flexible spending.
  • The remaining amount is your variable-spending pool.
  • The app converts the remaining pool into a daily allowance.
  • Actual spending is compared with the plan and projected to month-end.
